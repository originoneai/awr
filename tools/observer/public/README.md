# Owner-provided brand assets

The AWR logo was supplied by the project owner. The color exports retain the supplied colors on a transparent background. The white exports are pure-white silhouettes of exactly the same shape: their RGB channels are filled with white while the color artwork's alpha channel is preserved unchanged.

- `awr-logo-color-{72,144,288}.png`: color marks for the light theme, exported at 72, 144 and 288 pixels wide.
- `awr-logo-white-{72,144,288}.png`: matching white marks for the dark theme.
- `awr-favicon-{32,256}.png`: browser icons at their named square sizes.
- `awr-apple-touch-180.png`: the 180 × 180 Apple touch icon.

Only the browser-ready exports are committed; the larger master artwork remains outside the repository. All images are served from the Observer's own origin.
